# Archival note: revision 1.2.0

Sole author: Deep Bhattacharjee. DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23608134.

This research preprint is prepared for possible Annals of Mathematics submission; it is not an accepted journal article. It classifies all extremal even-intersecting lattice-path families by noncrossing perfect matchings, and all equality cases of the binary-word inequality by the stated partial matchings. The main proof applies to every length and is independent of computation. The accompanying Lean checks concern finite ranges only.

The matching editable package, vector figures, original verification sources, independent geometric-edge verifier, exact execution records and audit are released at https://github.com/creelie/Paper_3/releases/tag/v1.2.0 . Original preprint: https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00474v1 . Preceding archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23552911 . The final single ZIP is assembled only after this version's DOI is published and the PDF checksum is confirmed.

This revision adds journal formatting, explicit coordinate scope for the cell lemma, a precise finite-formalization disclosure, independently written geometric-edge checks, proof audit, BibTeX and source provenance. It credits the preceding resolution rather than claiming a new discovery of the same theorem.
