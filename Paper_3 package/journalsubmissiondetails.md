## Disclosure and scope

The paper proves the Catalan classification of maximum even-intersecting North-East lattice-path families. Its complete written induction classifies all equality cases of the underlying weighted binary-word inequality by noncrossing partial matchings, and excludes the exceptional pair in every admissible grouping.

Formal-check scope: The Lean checks cover stated finite ranges, including path-family classification for n at most 3. The all-length classification is justified by the written induction and is not claimed to be fully formalised in Lean.
