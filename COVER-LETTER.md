Editors, Annals of Mathematics

Please consider "Even-intersecting families of lattice paths and the Catalan numbers," by Deep Bhattacharjee, for publication.

The paper classifies every extremal family in the lattice-path analogue of Eventown: a family of 2^n North-East paths from (0,0) to (n,n), with even pairwise numbers of common edges, arises from a unique noncrossing perfect matching of its step positions. This proves Shankar's Conjecture 4.1 (arXiv:2607.23117v1) and gives exactly C_n extremal families. More generally, all equality cases of the underlying weighted inequality are classified by noncrossing partial matchings with no unmatched point under an arc.

The proof is elementary and supplied in full, including a reproof of the original inequality. Its rigidity lemma includes an exceptional pair, which a separate grouping argument excludes. The general result uses no exhaustive computation. Exact geometric-edge and SAT checks accompany it; the core-Lean development verifies finite ranges only, as explicitly disclosed.
